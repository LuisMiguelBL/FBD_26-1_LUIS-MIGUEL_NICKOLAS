# Domain Description: MediFlow

MediFlow is a medical clinic management system designed to organize and streamline the daily operations of a clinic. It centralizes control over key information related to patients, appointments, physicians and attendances, making workflows more efficient and reducing reliance on paper-based processes.

## Main Features

### Appointment Scheduling

The system allows appointments to be booked, rescheduled or cancelled, and provides a view of each physician's agenda. This helps prevent scheduling conflicts and improves the overall organization of patient care.

### Patient Registration

Patient records store information such as full name, CPF, phone number, address, health insurance plan and appointment history. All data is centralized and can be accessed quickly whenever needed.

### Electronic Medical Records

Physicians can register medical information for each visit, including reported symptoms, diagnosis, requested exams and attendance history. This allows the patient's health progress to be tracked over time.

### Medical Prescriptions

The system supports the creation of medical prescriptions, allowing physicians to record and print prescriptions that are also saved to the patient's history.

### Financial Control

The system enables the clinic to record payments, track which appointments have been paid, manage insurance-covered visits and generate billing reports.

### Staff and Physician Management

The system supports the registration of physicians and other staff members, allowing the definition of medical specialties and the organization of work schedules.

### Reports

The system can generate reports with key operational data, such as the number of appointments completed, billing for a given period and total patients seen. These reports support decision-making and clinic planning.
